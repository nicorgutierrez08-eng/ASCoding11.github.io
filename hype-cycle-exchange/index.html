How it works

  • Everyone starts with $10,000. Trade only while the timer is running.
  • Buy if you think a stock will rise. Sell shares you don't own to short it and profit if it falls.
  • Buy if you think a stock will rise, and sell shares you own to lock in gains. Short selling is turned off in this game.
  • You can't spend more cash than you have, and your shorts can't be worth more than your whole account.
  • After trading closes, the host reveals the year's news and new prices. Open the News tab for every story and the Markets tab for charts.
  • Taxes: when you sell for a profit you pay tax on the gain. Shares held through fewer than 2 years of results pay 30% short-term tax; shares held 2 or more years pay 15% long-term tax. Profits on shorts are always short-term.
  • Losses aren't taxed. They're saved and lower the tax on your next gains.
  • The leaderboard ranks everyone by after-tax value: what you'd keep if you sold everything today.
  • Taxes are turned off in this game, so you keep every dollar of profit.
  • If a company goes bankrupt its stock goes to $0: owners lose everything, and short sellers profit on the full sale price.
