How to play
  • Your turn: type something that fits the category (Countries, Animals, Things in a kitchen…) and press Enter. Wrong guesses cost nothing but time, so keep trying.
  • No repeats: anything already said in this category doesn't count. Spelling, accents, capitals and "the" don't matter, and well-known other names count.
  • The puffer: it swells while the player whose turn it is thinks. If it's still swelling when the time runs out, it pops and that player loses a life. Three lives each.
  • Faster and faster: every answer makes the next turn a little shorter, until someone pops. Then a new round starts with a new category, and the category also changes after a few answers.
  • Keys: Enter sends, M mutes.

      ? How to play
      • Take turns around the ring. When the puffer faces you and your name glows, type something that fits the category and press Enter (or Send).
      • Instant feedback: the box turns green when your answer fits. "Doesn't fit" or "Already said"? Just try another; only the clock can hurt you.
      • Pop! If the puffer is still swelling when your time runs out, it pops on you and you lose a life. Lose all three and you're out.
      • Shorter fuses: each answer makes the next turn a bit quicker. After a pop, the next round starts with a new category and the full fuse again.
      • Online: Quick match, or a room code your friends type in (or the invite link). Bots can fill a small room. Solo practice plays against bots on this device.
