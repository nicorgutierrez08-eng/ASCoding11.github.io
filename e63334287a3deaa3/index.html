Word game

Deepword

A daily word dive: the rarer your answer, the deeper you go.

  • Online · 2 to 12
  • Daily

A daily word dive. Each day brings eight open-ended prompts, the same for everyone, and the rarer your answer, the deeper you sink, from the sunlit surface down to the trench. Then take it online in Blowfish Brawl, where an answer someone else already sent puffs you up.

  • A share card with emoji squares, a streak, a best depth and a calendar of your month
  • Blowfish Brawl for 2 to 12 players: one prompt for the room, a shrinking timer, an answer someone already sent puffs you up, and three puffs pops you; bots fill small rooms
  • Practice with random prompts any time

Type your answer and press Enter. Works with a keyboard or a phone's own keyboard.

Puzzle

Gridpop

Drop blocks on an 8 × 8 grid and pop full lines.

  • Solo
  • Saves every move

A block-placement puzzle on an 8 × 8 board. Drag three pieces at a time onto the grid and fill whole rows or columns to pop them. Pieces can't be turned, and clearing again within a few moves builds a combo. The game ends when nothing in your tray fits anywhere.

  • 41 fixed pieces, from single squares to 3 × 3 blocks, Ls, Ts, zigzags and staircases, dealt three at a time with big pieces kept rare
  • Rows and columns clear together, and more lines in one move score much more (10, 30, 60, 100, 150, 210)
  • A combo multiplier that keeps going as long as you clear again within your next three placements
  • A preview shows where the piece will land and lights up the lines it would clear
  • Your game is saved after every move, with a best score kept in your browser

Plays with a mouse, a touch screen or the keyboard (1 2 3 to pick, arrows to move, Enter to place).

Puzzle

2048

Slide and join tiles to 2048, or go big on 6 × 6.

  • Solo
  • 4 modes

The number-sliding puzzle on a 4 × 4 board. Slide every tile at once, join matching numbers into one tile worth double, and keep the board from filling up long enough to build a 2048 tile. After you win you can keep going for 4096 and beyond. Four modes: Classic, a 6 × 6 Mega board, and College and Dessert, where the tiles climb a ladder of colleges or desserts instead of numbers.

  • Arrow keys or WASD (change them in Controls), or swipe on a phone; one move per swipe
  • Smooth slides, a pop on every join, a floating "+N" by the score, and confetti at 2048
  • Strict mode (on by default) is the game with no Undo; switch it off for a one-step Undo
  • Each mode keeps its own game in progress, best score and leaderboard
  • The game in progress, best score and settings save in this browser, so you can close the tab and finish later

Plays with the keyboard, a mouse drag or a swipe.

Parkour race

Spire

Race up a random tower. Mind the red, and grab the coins.

  • Online · up to 12
  • Solo too

A parkour race up a tower stacked from random sections, gentle at the bottom and nasty near the top: beams, spinning bars, vanishing tiles, ice, launch pads, gusts of wind and lasers. Every landing between sections is a checkpoint, and anything glowing red sends you back to it. Everyone in the room climbs the same tower against one clock, which runs twice as fast once someone reaches the top.

  • Online rooms for up to 12 climbers: Quick match drops you into a public tower, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • A new tower every round from 23 hand-built sections, each one checked by a bot that climbs it with the real movement, and a gold shortcut for the brave
  • Your place in the race beside the clock, split times at every landing, and a height bar showing where everyone is
  • Coins along the way and hats to spend them on (or unlock by levelling up), from a party hat to a propeller cap
  • Checkpoints can be turned off for the classic climb, where red means the bottom
  • Obby mode: 49 stages over seven themed worlds, and practice for any single section

Best with a keyboard and mouse (rebindable keys); phones get a joystick and buttons. Press Quick match, play in a room with friends, or climb on your own.

Racing

Slipstream

22-car races on real circuits. Draft, brake late, pass.

  • Online · up to 22
  • Bots

A 3D open-wheel racer with a full 22-car grid. Pick one of 11 teams, wait for the five red lights, then fight for the racing line through braking zones, slipstreams and boosts. Every seat nobody takes is driven by a bot, so every race starts with all 22 cars.

  • Online rooms for up to 22 drivers: each person picks a team and a seat, and bots fill every empty seat, including your teammate's
  • Quick race drops you into a public lobby, or pick a room code and send friends the invite link
  • If someone leaves mid-race a bot takes over their car, and if the host leaves another player takes over the bots
  • 23 real Grand Prix circuits laid out from map data, from Monaco, Spa and Monza to night races in Singapore and Las Vegas, plus three originals, over 3, 5 or 10 laps
  • A screaming V10 engine note that climbs through the gears, crackles on the overrun and Doppler-shifts as rivals pass
  • Slipstream, an ERS boost that recharges under braking, a live timing tower with gaps, fastest laps and four cameras
  • DRS like F1: from lap 2, get within a second of the car ahead at the detection line and open the rear wing in the zone
  • Keyboard, gamepad (analog stick and triggers) or an analog touch steering pad
  • F1-style track limits: going off deletes your lap, three strikes brings the black-and-white flag, then 5-second penalties; cutting a corner to gain time is a penalty straight away
  • Practice against 21 bots on Easy, Normal or Hard

Plays with a keyboard, a gamepad, or touch controls on a phone. Press Quick race, play in a room with friends, or practice against bots.
