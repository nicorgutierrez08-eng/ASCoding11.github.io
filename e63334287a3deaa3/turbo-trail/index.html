How to play
  • Levels: reach the portal at the end of each level. Your time starts when you start moving, and a fall or anything glowing red sends you straight back to the start of the level.
  • Medals: beat a level's bronze, silver or gold time for one, two or three stars. Stars unlock faster speeds and trails in the Garage.
  • Full run: every level in a row on one clock. After each portal your split shows green if you're ahead of your best run, red if behind.
  • Pads: yellow arrows launch you forward, purple rings throw you up, and striped walls can be jumped off (jump again while touching one).
  • Controls: WASD runs (relative to the camera), Space jumps (hold to keep hopping), R restarts the level, T restarts a full run. Click the game to look around with the mouse; Esc frees it. The wheel zooms.
  • Touch: the stick runs, the green button jumps, the arrow restarts the level, and dragging anywhere else turns the camera.
