How to play
  • Escape each room by reaching the green EXIT. The doors in the way open with a key, a keycard, a keypad code, buttons pressed in the right order, a block pushed onto a plate, or a fuse.
  • Red and glowing (lava, lasers, hot burners, spinning bars) sends you back to your last checkpoint. Keys, codes and opened doors stay done.
  • Stars: one for escaping, one for beating the target time, one for finding the level's hidden gem.
  • Keys: WASD moves, Space jumps, E uses buttons and keypads, R goes back to your checkpoint, click to aim with the mouse (Esc frees it), the wheel zooms, Shift locks the camera, V switches to first person.
  • Touch: the stick moves, drag anywhere else to look, pinch to zoom. USE lights up green when you're next to something you can use.
  • Online: everyone plays the same level at the same time, each with their own copy of the puzzles. The first person in the room picks the level; when everyone's out, the next one starts.
