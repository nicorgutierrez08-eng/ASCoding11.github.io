How to play

Pieces drop into a well ten squares wide. Slide and turn them so they fill complete rows: a full row vanishes and everything above it drops down.

Clear four rows with one piece for a QUAD! Twist the T piece into a snug gap for a T-spin, worth even more. Quads and T-spin clears in a row score back-to-back ×1.5, and clearing with piece after piece builds a combo.

The faint outline shows where the piece will land. Hold stashes a piece for later (once per piece). Every 10 rows the level goes up and pieces fall faster. If a new piece has no room to appear, it's over.

Every piece is made of sand in one colour. Steer it as usual, but the moment it touches the floor or the pile it crumbles into grains that tumble and slide downhill.

When sand of one colour joins up from the left wall to the right wall (grains touching side to side or top to bottom; corners don't count) that whole patch dissolves. Sand above falls in, and can finish another link: each extra clear in the same chain multiplies the points, and clearing several colours in one chain earns a bonus.

The level rises every 3,000 grains cleared. Keep the pile under the red line: if sand is still above it a second after the next piece is due, the game ends.
