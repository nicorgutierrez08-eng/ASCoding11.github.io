      How to play
      1. Each show is a few rounds. In a race, cross the finish before the qualifying spots fill up. In a survival, stay on until the clock runs out.
      2. Fall in the slime during a race and you pop back at the last checkpoint. In survivals and the final, a fall knocks you out.
      3. The final is on the honeycomb: tiles vanish a moment after anyone touches them. Keep moving and be the last one up.
      4. Dive for a burst of speed, and grab people to slow them down. Jump the low sweepers; you fit under the high ones, so don't jump into them.
