? How to play

The goal

Drop fruit into the jar. When two of the same fruit touch, they merge into the next fruit up. Merges set off more merges, and every merge in a chain scores.

Fruit, smallest to biggest

Cherry, strawberry, grape, tangerine, persimmon, apple, pear, peach, pineapple, melon, watermelon. You only ever get the first five to drop.

Points per merge

Two watermelons that touch burst for a +200 bonus and leave room in the jar.

Game over

If any fruit sits above the red dashed line for a second and a half, the jar overflows. A fruit you just dropped doesn't count while it's falling in.
