How to play
  • Tower: a stack of random sections, gentle at the bottom and harder near the top. Climb the truss between sections to reach the next one. The clock runs twice as fast once someone reaches the top.
  • Checkpoints: each landing between sections saves your place. Glowing red, or dropping out of your section, puts you back on it. With checkpoints off, red sends you to the bottom and a fall just lands you lower down.
  • Look out for ice (you slide), launch pads (they throw you the way the arrows point), gusts (wait for a lull), and gold shortcuts that are quicker but harder.
  • Coins float along the way. Spend them on hats, or level up to unlock more.
  • Obby: 49 stages over seven worlds. Every stage starts on a checkpoint, and your furthest stage is saved.
  • Controls: WASD moves (relative to the camera), Space jumps (hold to keep jumping), click the game to aim the camera with the mouse (a crosshair shows in the middle, you face where you aim as you move, and Esc frees the mouse), the wheel zooms, Shift keeps you facing the camera's way even standing still, V switches between first and third person (scrolling all the way in does too).
  • Touch: the stick moves, drag anywhere else to turn the camera, pinch to zoom, and the eye button switches to first person.
