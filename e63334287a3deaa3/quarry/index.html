An endless world of hills, forests, deserts and snowy peaks, made of blocks you can dig out and build with. Craft tools at a crafting table, smelt ore in a furnace, raise animals for food and hold out against the zombies at night.

Survival: punch a tree for logs, turn them into planks, make a crafting table and lay out a wooden pickaxe in its 3×3 grid. Stone tools mine iron, iron tools mine gold and diamonds. Smelt ore and cook meat in a furnace with coal or wood. Eat to keep your hunger bar up, and get somewhere bright before night, when zombies come out, or sleep through it in a bed.

The Nether: build an obsidian frame (4 wide, 5 tall) and light it with flint and steel. Inside are nether fortresses, where flares drop ember rods. The End: combine ember powder with a void pearl (from voidwalkers) to make Seeker eyes, follow them to a stronghold, fill its portal frames, then beat the Void Wyrm.

Farming: hoe grass into farmland near water and plant seeds, carrots, potatoes, and pumpkin or melon seeds; sugar cane grows by water. Hold an animal's food to lead it, and feed two of them to breed a baby: wheat for cows and sheep, carrots or potatoes for pigs, seeds for hens. Shear sheep, milk cows with a bucket, and bake a cake.

Fighting: skeletons shoot arrows, spiders climb walls and Boomshrooms explode, so craft a bow and armour. Kills and ores give XP; spend levels at an enchanting table ringed with bookshelves. Rain and thunderstorms roll in on their own, and lightning starts fires.

Saplings from leaves grow into trees. Redstone ore, deep down, gives redstone dust for wiring levers, buttons, pressure plates, torches, repeaters, lamps and doors.
