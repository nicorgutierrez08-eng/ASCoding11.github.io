    How to play
    1. Props get 30 seconds to hide while the hunters wait in a locked room. Aim at any object and click to become it, then park yourself where it looks at home.
    2. Lock rotation to keep facing the way the real ones do while you look around. Keep still: moving gives you away.
    3. Every few seconds every prop lets out a ping, so the hunters can hear roughly where you are. Pings come faster in the last minute.
    4. Hunters shoot anything suspicious. Hitting a disguised prop damages it; hitting a real object costs you health.
    5. Hunters win if every prop is found. Props win if one of them is still standing when time runs out. Everyone takes a turn hunting.
