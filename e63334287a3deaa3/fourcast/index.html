Four words, nine tries

Four hidden five-letter words, nine guesses. Every word you guess goes on all four boards at once, so one guess can help with every word.

Green: right letter, right spot. Yellow: in that word, but elsewhere. Dark: not in that word.

  • A board stops once you solve it; keep going until all four are done.
  • Each key is split into four corners, one per board, in reading order: board 1 top left, 2 top right, 3 bottom left, 4 bottom right.
  • Solve all four within nine guesses to win. Repeated letters work as in Hunch: greens first, then yellows for as many as the word has.
  • On a computer, just type: Enter checks a word, Backspace deletes.

A new puzzle every day at midnight, your time. Unlimited mode is for practice and doesn't change your stats.
