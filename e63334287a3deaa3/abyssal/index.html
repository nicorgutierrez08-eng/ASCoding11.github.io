    How to play

    Sink as deep as you can

    • 7 prompts each asks you to name one thing in a category.
    • 25 seconds per prompt. Wrong guesses cost time, not points.
    • Rarer answers land in deeper tiers and score more.
    • Too Clever is the "obscure" pick everyone else also thought of. It scores just 15.
    • Typos are fine. Close misspellings are autocorrected, and near misses get a "Did you mean…?" you can accept.
    • 1 point sinks you 10 m. A perfect 700 reaches the trench floor at 7,000 m.
