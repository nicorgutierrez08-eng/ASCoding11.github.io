How to play Paused

  • Every move slides all the tiles as far as they go: use ↑ ← ↓ → (or the other keys in Controls)swipe up, down, left or right.
  • Two tiles with the same number that bump together join into one tile worth both. The new tile's number is added to your score.
  • A tile only joins once per move, and the pair closest to the wall you slide toward joins first.
  • After each move that changes the board, a new 2 (sometimes a 4) drops into an empty square.
  • Build a 2048 tile to win, then keep going for 4096 and beyond if you like.
  • The game ends when the board is full and no two touching tiles match.
  • Four modes, each with its own board, best score and leaderboard: Classic is the 4 × 4 game, Mega is 6 × 6, and College and Dessert play exactly the same as Classic but the tiles climb a ladder of colleges or desserts instead of showing numbers (the number is still in the corner).
  • Your games save in this browser, one per mode, so you can close the tab and finish later.
