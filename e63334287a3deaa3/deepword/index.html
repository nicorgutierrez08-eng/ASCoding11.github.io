How to play
  • Daily Dive: the same eight prompts for everyone today. Type any valid answer; the fewer divers who gave it, the more points, and every point is 15 m of depth. You have 30 seconds of air per prompt.
  • Answers are checked against the game's own lists, and the input turns green when yours is on the list. Spelling, accents and "the" don't matter; well-known aliases count.
  • Zones: Sunlit to 200 m, Twilight to 1,000 m, Midnight to 4,000 m, Abyss to 6,000 m, then the Trench. A perfect dive reaches the deepest point in the ocean.
  • Blowfish Brawl: same prompt for everyone in the room, 10 seconds that shrink to 6. An answer nobody else sent first keeps you safe; a blank, an invalid answer or a duplicate puffs you up. Three puffs and you pop. Last one swimming wins.
  • Keys: Enter sends, Esc pauses in Practice, M mutes.
