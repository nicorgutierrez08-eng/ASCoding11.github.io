How to play

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Pieces can't be turned, so every shape has to go in the way it comes.
  • Fill a whole row or column and it pops. Rows and columns clear at the same time, and more lines in one move score a lot more.
  • Combos: clear again within your next three placements (up to two misses in between) and the combo multiplier keeps climbing.
  • Three at a time: place all three pieces, in any order, and a new set arrives.
  • The game ends when none of your pieces fits anywhere. Your game is saved after every move.
