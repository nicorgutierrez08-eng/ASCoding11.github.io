Hard problems I solved

Networking

Smooth multiplayer on school Wi-Fi

Position updates arrive late and in bursts. netsmooth.js measures each player’s lag and plays them back from a short history, so other players move smoothly instead of jumping.

Performance

3D on cheap school laptops

autores.js watches the frame rate in every three.js game and lowers the resolution a tenth at a time when frames drop below 45 a second, then raises it again once the game is smooth.

Security

Sealed safety log and feedback

Chat reports and feedback are encrypted in the browser to an admin public key (ECDH P-256 and AES-GCM). Only the admin’s two codes can open them, even though they’re stored in a shared database.

Product

Parties, leaderboards, and a shop

Friends form a party and jump into any multiplayer game together. Leaderboards track play time and personal bests. Skins are sold through Stripe and stay with your Google account on every device.

Data

My own analytics

A 114-line tracker writes anonymous page views, sessions and engaged time (paused after two idle minutes) to Firestore. A private dashboard shows retention, sources and search clicks.

Reach

Every game works on a phone

Each game draws its own touch controls and shows them only on touch screens, so the keyboard-and-mouse version stays unchanged. A separate phone page lists every game as a tile.
